A word about us

About Poet's Inn

Nestled in the serene Pahalgam Valley, Poet's Inn offers a tranquil escape where nature and comfort blend seamlessly. Surrounded by the breathtaking Lidder River and majestic mountains, our boutique inn provides an intimate and personalised experience for travellers. Whether you're here to explore, immerse yourself in local culture, or simply unwind, we offer a peaceful retreat that feels like home. Welcome to an oasis of tranquillity, where every guest is treated like family.
